# 👁️ RAKE BAN EYES
## PRIVACY POLICY

### 1. Information We Collect
When you submit a report, we may collect:
- Telegram User ID
- Report category
- Group or Channel link
- Report description
- Screenshot evidence

### 2. Purpose of Collection
Information is collected to receive, review, and manage reports.

### 3. Data Storage
Reports are stored in the bot's database for administrative review. Authorized administrators can access submitted reports.

### 4. Information Sharing
We do not publish submitted reports publicly. Information may be reviewed by authorized administrators.

### 5. Evidence Submission
Please submit only relevant evidence you are authorized to share. Avoid unnecessary personal information.

### 6. Data Retention
Reports may remain stored until an administrator deletes them.

### 7. Accurate Reporting
Users must provide truthful and accurate information. False or malicious reports are not permitted.

### 8. Telegram Disclaimer
RAKE BAN EYES is an independent reporting tool and is not officially affiliated with Telegram. Telegram independently decides whether to take action.

### 9. Contact
For privacy-related questions, contact the RAKE BAN EYES administrators.
